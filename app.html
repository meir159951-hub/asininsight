Where do I get this file?

You need the Detail Page Sales and Traffic report from Amazon Seller Central.

  1. In Seller Central, go to Reports → Business Reports
  2. Click "By Child Item" under "Detail Page Sales and Traffic"
  3. Set the date range to last 30 days
  4. Click Download (.csv) and the file downloads automatically
Tip: Any 30-day period works. Shorter windows give less reliable results. If you've never exported this report, it takes about 30 seconds to find.
