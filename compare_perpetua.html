SellerCopilot vs Perpetua

Perpetua is autopilot. SellerCopilot structurally cannot push a change to your Amazon account.

Perpetua positions itself as a "self-driving car" for Amazon Ads. That is the explicit category split between us. If you want a tool that auto-applies bid changes and campaign restructures, Perpetua is the established choice. If you have been burned by a tool that auto-applied a change you did not want, this page exists for you.

Last reviewed: 2026-05-09. Sources: Perpetua public pages and verbatim third-party reviews.

The category split

Autopilot vs approval-first.

Perpetua's core promise is hands-off automation. From their own positioning, the AI runs the campaigns and the seller monitors dashboards. SellerCopilot sits on the opposite end of the spectrum: every change is queued in your dashboard with the math, the evidence, and a one-sentence reason you might still reject it. You apply each one yourself. There is no apply path in our codebase. The promise is the architecture, not a brand line.

Both models are valid for some sellers. They are not interchangeable. If you do not want to look at your account every week, Perpetua's model fits you. If you have been bitten by an autopilot bid change at 2 a.m. that took ten days to recover from, ours might.

Where Perpetua is stronger

What we will not pretend.

1. Hands-off operation if you trust the autopilot

Perpetua optimizes 24/7 without the seller's involvement. SellerCopilot requires you to read each recommendation and click apply. If your goal is "log in once a month and check the dashboard," Perpetua is the right shape.

2. Track record at scale

Perpetua serves enterprise brands with deep ad budgets. They have shipped at scale for years. SellerCopilot is earlier-stage and targets sellers in the $5K-$50K monthly spend band.

3. Larger feature surface for the price

Perpetua bundles DSP, sponsored video, sponsored brands, and other ad types. SellerCopilot is currently focused on Sponsored Products. If you advertise across all Amazon ad types, Perpetua's bundle saves vendor sprawl.

When to pick which

Honest decision rules.

Pick Perpetua if

  • Your spend is above $50K/mo and you need DSP + Sponsored Brands + Sponsored Display in one platform.
  • You want the AI to run campaigns continuously without your daily involvement.
  • You have a team that monitors dashboards rather than a solo operator who reviews each change.

Pick SellerCopilot if

  • Your spend is $5K-$50K/mo and Perpetua's price tier is wrong for you.
  • You have been burned by an autopilot tool and want a vendor that structurally cannot repeat the failure.
  • You want a decision audit trail that reads like a ledger an accountant or board can review.
  • You want flat pricing instead of percent-of-spend or enterprise contracts.
The category split is real. We are not "Perpetua but cheaper." We are a different product for a different operator.
