## Full Description

### The problem every Amazon seller has

Your Business Report has 20+ columns. CTR, CVR, sessions, Buy Box percentage, ACOS, review count — all staring at you with no explanation of which one actually matters right now.

Most sellers check 3 of them and guess the rest. Or they pay a consultant $150/hour to tell them what their own data already says.

### What ASINInsight does

You upload the Detail Page Sales and Traffic by Child Item CSV (the standard Business Report from Seller Central). In under 30 seconds, ASINInsight:

1. **Identifies the single biggest problem** — CTR below the 0.20% floor, CVR below 8%, ACOS above 35%, Buy Box below 90%, session drops, review issues
2. **Calculates the monthly revenue impact** — not just "your CTR is low" but "this is costing you ~$340/month vs. category average"
3. **Gives you a numbered action plan** — specific steps ranked by impact, starting with the one fix that moves the needle most

### Who this is for

- Solo sellers managing 5-50 ASINs who want weekly clarity without hiring an analyst
- Brand managers running weekly listing audits across a catalog
- Amazon VAs and consultants who need fast diagnosis across multiple client accounts (Agency plan)

### What makes it different

Most Amazon tools give you dashboards. ASINInsight gives you a **verdict** — one primary issue, one action plan, one place to start. The goal is to spend less time analyzing and more time fixing.

No Amazon SP-API connection required. No permissions, no OAuth. Just the CSV you already pull every week.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need to connect my Amazon account?**  
A: No. You upload the CSV from Seller Central manually. No API connection, no permissions.

**Q: What file exactly do I need?**  
A: Reports → Business Reports → Detail Page Sales and Traffic by Child Item. Set to 30 days, download CSV.

**Q: Does it work for all Amazon marketplaces?**  
A: Yes — the Business Report format is the same across US, UK, EU, and other marketplaces.

**Q: Is my data safe?**  
A: Your CSV is processed and deleted immediately. Nothing is stored on our servers.

**Q: What if my diagnosis seems wrong?**  
A: The tool uses the same diagnostic thresholds used by experienced Amazon consultants (0.20% CTR floor, 8% CVR threshold, etc.). Edge cases exist — contact support with your numbers and we'll investigate.
