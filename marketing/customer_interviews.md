# Customer Interview Script & Recruiting Playbook
*Week 1 priority. Run before writing any marketing copy.*

---

## Pre-call Setup

- **Length:** 30 minutes, recorded with consent (Riverside or Fathom).
- **Incentive:** $50 Amazon gift card OR free 6-month Pro account in exchange. **Don't offer cash** (skews respondents toward the desperate).
- **Pre-screen before booking:** *"Do you sell on Amazon as your primary income, and do you have access to your Seller Central Sales and Traffic reports?"* If no to either, don't book.
- **Goal:** 5 completed interviews in Week 1. Capture verbatim phrases, willingness-to-pay signal, and trust gates.

---

## The 10 Questions (Sequenced)

### Opening — Rapport (1 question)

**Q1.** *"Before we get into anything about tools — just tell me about your store. What do you sell, how long have you been at it, and is this your full-time thing or a side project?"*

- **Signal:** Specificity. Real sellers name their category, give revenue ranges unprompted, and reference SKUs by ASIN or rank.
- **Disqualifier:** Pre-revenue, "I'm researching what to sell," or dropshipping-only. ICP is sellers with at least 6 months of FBA history and live SKUs.
- **Probe if thin:** *"What's a typical month look like in revenue ballpark — $5K, $50K, $500K?"*

---

### Current Workflow Discovery (3 questions)

**Q2.** *"Walk me through what happened the last time you noticed something was off with one of your listings — sales, conversion, traffic, anything. What did you do, step by step?"*

- **Signal:** Mention of Business Reports, Brand Analytics, or downloading the Sales & Traffic CSV. Mention of cross-checking session counts vs. unit-session percentage. Time spent (hours, not minutes).
- **Disqualifier:** *"I don't really track it"* or *"my VA handles all that and I just look at the bank account."* ICP touches the data themselves at least monthly.
- **Probe:** *"Once you saw the drop, how long until you figured out the cause? And how confident were you in the cause when you finally landed on one?"*

**Q3.** *"What tools do you currently pay for, and what do you use each one for? Even the ones you bought and stopped using."*

- **Signal:** Helium 10, Jungle Scout, Data Dive, SellerBoard, ManyChat. **The graveyard of cancelled subscriptions tells you what didn't stick.**
- **Disqualifier:** Pays for nothing, uses no tools — likely too small or too cheap to convert.
- **Probe:** *"Of those, which one would hurt the most to lose tomorrow? Why that one?"*

**Q4.** *"When you pull the Sales and Traffic report from Seller Central — or any report really — what do you actually do with the file once it's downloaded?"*

- **Signal:** Pivot tables in Excel, manual filtering, *"I stare at it and feel overwhelmed,"* paying a VA to summarize. Any answer that involves friction is gold.
- **Disqualifier:** *"I never download those, I just look at the dashboard."* This person doesn't feel the pain ASINInsight removes.
- **Probe:** *"Roughly how long does that whole process take you, and how often do you actually finish it before getting distracted?"*

---

### Emotional Pain Language (2 questions)

**Q5.** *"Tell me about the worst week or month you've had as a seller in the last year. Not financially worst necessarily — the most frustrating. What was going on?"*

- **Signal:** Phrases like *"tanked overnight," "couldn't figure out why," "Amazon support was useless," "I was up at 2am refreshing."* Write these down verbatim — they become your ad copy.
- **Disqualifier:** No emotional charge, no specific incident recalled. Either they're not invested or not your buyer.
- **Probe:** *"What was the part that kept you up at night specifically — the money, the not-knowing, something else?"*

**Q6.** *"If you could wave a wand and have one thing about running your Amazon business be magically easier, what would it be? Don't think about what's realistic."*

- **Signal:** Diagnosis, attribution, *"knowing what to do next."* If they say *"more sales"* they're not thinking about the workflow problem; reframe.
- **Disqualifier:** *"Lower fees"* or *"less competition"* — these aren't workflow pains, they're market complaints. Not your buyer.
- **Probe:** *"Pretend the wand worked. What would your Monday morning look like differently?"*

---

### Willingness-to-Pay (2 questions, no anchoring)

**Q7.** *"Of all the Amazon software you've ever paid for, what's the most you've ever paid per month, and was it worth it?"*

- **Signal:** They name a number ($99 Helium 10 Diamond, $399 Data Dive, etc.) and either justify or regret it. The justification language is your sales copy.
- **Disqualifier:** Has never paid more than $30/mo for any tool — likely too price-sensitive for $49 Pro.
- **Probe:** *"What specifically made it worth it / not worth it? Was it features, time saved, money made?"*

**Q8.** *"Imagine a tool that took your Sales and Traffic CSV and gave you back a ranked list — 'fix these 5 things in this order, here's why each one is hurting you.' No Amazon login required, no SP-API token, file deleted instantly after the audit. What would that be worth to you per month, if it actually worked?"*

- **Signal:** Any number. **The number itself matters less than how fast they answer.** Fast answer = real demand. Long pause + *"I dunno, maybe $20?"* = polite dismissal.
- **Disqualifier:** *"I wouldn't pay for that, I can do it myself in Excel."* They might be right or wrong, but they're not a customer.
- **Probe:** *"What would have to be true about the output for you to pay [their number]? What would make you pay double that?"*

---

### Trust Gate (1 question)

**Q9.** *"Imagine you found this tool tomorrow on Reddit or in a newsletter. What would have to be on the landing page — or what would someone have to say about it — for you to actually upload your CSV and try it? What would make you nope out instantly?"*

- **Signal:** Mentions of *"data privacy," "doesn't connect to my Seller Central," "real seller using it," "before/after numbers," "free trial without credit card."* Each one is a landing-page requirement.
- **Disqualifier:** None — every answer here is useful even from non-ICP.
- **Probe:** *"Have you ever signed up for an Amazon tool and immediately regretted it? What was the red flag in hindsight?"*

---

### Close + Referral (1 question)

**Q10.** *"This was super helpful. Last thing — who else do you know who runs an Amazon business and has the same kind of headaches you've been describing? Even one name would help me a ton, and I'm happy to mention you sent me, or not, whichever you prefer."*

- **Signal:** Names, Slack groups, mastermind names. **Any specific intro is worth ~10x a cold message.**
- **Disqualifier:** None.
- **Probe:** *"What's the best way to reach them — would you mind a quick intro, or should I just cold message?"*

## Where to Find These 5 Sellers

### Channel 1: r/FulfillmentByAmazon weekly "Help" threads
Don't post asking for interviews — instead, find sellers asking specific Sales & Traffic questions in the last 30 days, give them a genuinely useful comment-level answer, then DM after 48 hours.

### Channel 2: Twitter/X
Search `("FBA" OR "Amazon seller") (sessions OR conversion OR ACOS) -filter:replies` filtered to last 30 days. Sellers tweeting frustrations are warmer leads than anyone in a forum.

### Channel 3: Two paid masterminds with Slack/Discord
Million Dollar Sellers and FBA High Rollers (FB group, then ask to join their off-FB chat). Lurk for 7 days, contribute one substantive answer, then post a recruiting message in the appropriate channel (#feedback, #beta, or DM mods first).

## Recruiting Message Template

For Reddit DM (adapt for Twitter/LinkedIn/FB):

> Hey [name] — saw your post last week about [specific thing they posted, e.g. "sessions dropping but BSR holding"]. Not pitching anything. I'm a solo founder building a tool that turns the Seller Central Sales & Traffic CSV into a ranked fix-list (no login, file stays in your browser). Doing 30-min calls with 8 sellers this week to make sure I'm not building the wrong thing. $50 Amazon gift card or 6 months free on the paid tier when it launches, your pick. Cool to chat? Here's my Calendly: [link]. Totally fine if not.

**Tone notes:**
- Lowercase "hey," no hype
- Name the **exact** thing they posted (proves you read)
- Lead with *"not pitching"*
- If you can't reference a specific post of theirs, **don't send it**

## After 5 Interviews — What to Do With the Data

1. **Pull every emotional phrase from Q5, Q6, Q9.** These are headlines.
2. **Tally the WTP numbers from Q8.** Plot the distribution. Is $49 above, at, or below the median? If below median, the price is a deal. If above, plan the trust copy carefully.
3. **Identify common workflow friction from Q4.** What CSV step gets stuck? That's your demo video's first 15 seconds.
4. **Cross-reference tool churn from Q3.** Which tools did everyone cancel? Position against them ("we're the [thing they kept] for [thing they cancelled]").
5. **List the trust requirements from Q9 in priority order.** Each becomes a hero-section bullet point.

## Anti-Patterns

- **Don't lead the witness.** Never say "we built X to solve Y." Let them describe Y unprompted.
- **Don't sell.** Even if they ask "can I see a demo?" — say *"yes, but after you finish telling me about your workflow"*. Demo skews their answers.
- **Don't interview employees of competitors.** Helium 10/Jungle Scout/AuditMyListing employees will give skewed answers.
- **Don't interview only happy customers** of any tool — the unhappy ones are your buyers.
