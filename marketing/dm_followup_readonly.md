# DM follow-up — for people who reply to the read-only posts

**Use when:** Someone comments on the Reddit / Facebook / LinkedIn post and engages with the trust-in-automation question.
**Goal:** Get them to look at a sample report or agree to a 15-min call. Never include a signup link or a sales pitch in this first DM.

---

Hey [name], really appreciated your reply on the [Reddit / FB / LinkedIn] post.

Quick context on why I'm asking: I'm building a small tool for sellers in exactly your situation — it reads your Sponsored Products data and tells you what to change, but it physically can't touch your account. Every recommendation needs your click. The whole point is that you stay in control and have a clean log of every decision for your accountant or agency.

Two questions if you have 5 minutes:

1. What's the one ad / PPC problem you'd most want a second pair of eyes on this week?
2. If I sent you a sample report based on your own CSV export from Seller Central, would you actually look at it?

No pitch, no signup, no link. Just want to know if what I'm building would have caught the kind of thing you described.
