# Competitive Deep-Dive — Astra vs AutoPilot vs SellerCopilot

> **Purpose:** Sharpen positioning by understanding the closest "AI-memory" competitors deeply.
> **Date:** 2026-05-11
> **Status:** Round 4 research

---

## TL;DR

The two competitors most likely to claim "AI memory" — **Astra by Sellrbox** and **AiHello AutoPilot** — both have **algorithmic learning** but neither has **conversational memory of decisions**. SellerCopilot's wedge survives this round, but needs to be articulated more precisely.

---

## 1. Astra by Sellrbox

### What it is
Cloud-based PPC automation with hourly bid changes and ML-based budget allocation.

### Pricing
- **Free tier:** Available, no credit card
- **Explorer / Professional / Enterprise** (specific prices not public)
- Likely $30-$300/mo range based on category

### Key features
- Automated hourly bid updates
- Dayparting (pause ads during low-conversion hours)
- Sponsored Products + Brands + Display support
- Algorithm adjusts daily budget based on historical spending data
- "Learns over time" — but at the algorithm level

### What "memory" means for Astra
**Astra's "memory" = ML model parameters that get tuned.**

- Bid floor for keyword X learned from past performance
- Budget pace adjusted from historical CPC + conversion patterns
- Dayparting schedule learned from time-of-day performance

**Crucially: This memory is opaque to the seller.** The seller can't ask: "What did you learn about my account last month?" The ML just does its thing.

### What's the moat for Astra
- Hourly bid speed (most tools update daily)
- Free tier hook
- Self-service onboarding

### What Astra does NOT do
- ❌ Conversational interface
- ❌ Decision log the seller can read
- ❌ Strategic memory ("don't scale SKU X below 25% margin")
- ❌ Memory of seller-specific reasoning (only data patterns)
- ❌ "Why did you do this?" explanations

### How SellerCopilot beats Astra
**Astra:** *"My algorithm adjusted bids based on 30-day patterns."*
**SellerCopilot:** *"I remember in March we tried bid +30% on this keyword and ACOS spiked to 52%, so I'm proposing only +12%. Want to confirm?"*

The first is automation. The second is a colleague.

## 2. AiHello AutoPilot

### What it is
"Set ACOS target and let it run" hands-off bid manager. Intraday bid micromanagement.

### Pricing
- **Custom** (for paid tiers)
- One mention: **$695/mo** for accounts up to $10K/mo ad spend
- This is **enterprise-tier pricing** — not the SMB sweet spot

### Key features
- 24/7 bid optimization
- Boosts bids during peak hours, reduces during off-peak
- Handles thousands to millions of keywords
- Full "AutoPilot" mode

### What "memory" means for AutoPilot
**AutoPilot's "memory" = continuous learning ML on bid response curves.**

- It learns when each keyword converts
- It learns time-of-day patterns
- It learns budget pace

**Same problem as Astra: opaque, algorithmic, not conversational.**

### What's the moat for AutoPilot
- Truly hands-off (no review needed)
- Intraday optimization speed
- Scale (millions of keywords)

### What AutoPilot does NOT do
- ❌ Conversational interface
- ❌ Strategic context ("we don't scale this SKU on weekends")
- ❌ Surface its decisions for review (it just acts)
- ❌ Affordable for $5K-$50K/mo ad spend sellers (priced for enterprise)
- ❌ Explain reasoning

### How SellerCopilot beats AutoPilot

**AutoPilot:** *"Target ACOS set. Bids will be optimized 24/7. Trust the algorithm."*

**SellerCopilot:** *"I'm going to propose 5 changes today. Each one I'll show you why. You approve. I'll remember your reasoning for next time."*

AutoPilot sells **automation** to people who want to disappear from PPC.
SellerCopilot sells **partnership** to people who want to stay informed but not buried in details.

**Different buyer.** Same category, different buyer.

## 3. The Real Threat — Hidden Pattern Across Both

Both Astra and AutoPilot share a critical architectural choice:

> **They optimize TACTICS (bids, budgets, dayparts).**
> **They don't engage with STRATEGY (what to scale, what to kill, why).**

This is by design — automation tools want to be set-it-and-forget-it. Their value prop is "less work for you."

**SellerCopilot inverts this:**
> **We optimize STRATEGY through conversation. Tactics are easy. Strategy is hard.**

### Different value props comparison

| Tool | "What I do for you" |
|---|---|
| Astra | "I update your bids hourly so you don't have to" |
| AutoPilot | "I run your PPC 24/7 in the background" |
| Helium 10 Adtomic | "I give you rules and bulk operations to manage at scale" |
| Trellis | "I optimize ads + pricing + inventory together for enterprise brands" |
| **SellerCopilot** | **"I'm your strategic copilot. I remember every decision and learn your business. Talk to me like a partner."** |

These are **different products serving different psychological needs.**

## 4. Positioning Refined (after this analysis)

### What SellerCopilot is NOT
- ❌ NOT a faster bid optimizer (Astra does this)
- ❌ NOT a hands-off automation tool (AutoPilot does this)
- ❌ NOT enterprise PPC suite (Quartile, Pacvue do this)
- ❌ NOT a rule-based engine (Adtomic does this)

### What SellerCopilot IS
- ✅ A conversational AI agent that remembers your business
- ✅ A strategic copilot, not a tactical operator
- ✅ Designed for sellers who want to **stay informed and in control**, not disappear
- ✅ Honest about its reasoning (shows work, admits uncertainty)

### The one-sentence positioning

> **"SellerCopilot is the only PPC tool you can have a real conversation with — about your business, your decisions, your goals — and it remembers everything."**

### Who buys this
Sellers who:
- Want to stay informed about their PPC, not delegate it entirely
- Have been burned by agencies and don't trust black-box automation
- Want a colleague in the room, not a robot
- Are willing to pay $89/mo for clarity instead of $695/mo for automation

### Who DOESN'T buy this
- Pure efficiency seekers ("just optimize my bids, I don't want to talk")
- Enterprise brands needing 99.9% uptime + multi-channel
- Sellers who already have an in-house PPC manager (overlap of value)

## 6. Final Strategic Read

### Strengths of the SellerCopilot direction
- ✅ Real wedge that survives competitive analysis
- ✅ Different psychological need than competitors (partnership vs. automation)
- ✅ Anthropic Managed Agents removes most technical risk
- ✅ Pricing band ($89-149) is unoccupied in this category

### Risks (must address)
- ⚠️ "Conversational" interfaces test poorly with B2B users who want efficiency
- ⚠️ "Memory" is invisible — must show it in UI, not just claim it
- ⚠️ Sellers may default to brand they know (Helium 10 inertia)
- ⚠️ Amazon Ads Agent will eventually expand to SP

### Key validation question for Meir's surveys
Don't ask: *"Do you want a tool with memory?"* (everyone says yes)

Ask: *"When was the last time you wished your PPC tool understood something specific about your business that it ignored? What was it?"*

The answers tell us **what to remember first.** That's the product spec.
