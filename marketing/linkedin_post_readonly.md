# LinkedIn post — read-only PPC positioning

**Audience:** Brand operators, agency owners, ecommerce ops leaders.
**Tone:** Observation + question. Slightly more polished than Reddit/FB.

---

A pattern I keep seeing in conversations with Amazon sellers this quarter:

The bigger the brand, the less they trust ad automation.

Sellers under $50K/month want "set it and forget it." Sellers over $500K/month want to approve every meaningful change themselves — and most of them have a story about a tool that cost them five figures in a weekend.

Two questions for the brand operators and agencies in my network:

1. Where's the actual line where automation stops being a time-saver and starts being a risk?
2. If your ad tool was structurally incapable of changing anything without your sign-off — would that make you trust it more, or would it just feel like extra clicks?

Genuinely curious. Reply or DM — I'll summarize what I hear back in a follow-up post.
