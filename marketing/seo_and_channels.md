## AppSumo Economics — Run the Numbers

- **Sweet spot price:** $49–$79. Anything >$89 base usually rejected.
- **Revenue split:** AppSumo takes **70%**, founder gets **30%**.
- **At $69 LTD × 500 codes capped:** Gross $34,500, founder receives **~$10,350**.
- **60-day money-back guarantee:** all purchases refundable.
- **Distribution:** Pictory went 50 → 6,000 users in 2 months. Komodo Decks sold 6,000+ licenses.

**Verdict:** AppSumo is a one-time **audience injection + light cash injection**, not a primary revenue line. ASINInsight's **Agency tier** is a better LTD candidate than the Pro tier:
- Limits cannibalization of $49 Pro MRR
- $69 Agency LTD vs. $199/mo recurring = different value proposition
- Buyers are agencies (multi-client need), so 500-code cap matches small-agency density

**Recommendation:** Submit Agency-tier-only LTD at $69, capped at 500 codes, in GTM Week 9.

Sources: [AppSumo Selling Guide](https://sell.appsumo.com/e/one-stop-shop), [99signals AppSumo Lifetime Deals](https://www.99signals.com/appsumo-deals/)

## COSMO + Rufus — The 2026 Algorithm Reality

| System | What it does | Implication for ASINInsight |
|---|---|---|
| **A10** (legacy) | Mechanical ranking: sales velocity, conversion, CTR, lexical keyword match | Title is still highest-impact field |
| **COSMO** | AI-powered semantic intent matching; reads structured backend attributes | Backend keyword *quality* matters more than density |
| **Rufus** | Generative AI shopping assistant, 274M+ daily queries, $10B incremental sales, 60% higher conversion vs non-users | **Rufus shows 5 products, not 50** — being one of those 5 is now the game |

**Critical:** The action plan must explicitly map each fix to A10 / COSMO / Rufus signals. Otherwise ZonGuru (the only competitor naming these) outpositions ASINInsight on credibility.

**Headline copy that uses this:**
- *"Your listing isn't ranking? COSMO and Rufus changed what they reward in 2025. Here's what to fix first."*
- *"5 reasons your ASIN dropped after the 2025 algorithm update — and the fix order that recovers fastest."*
