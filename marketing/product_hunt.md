## Maker's First Comment

Hi Product Hunt 👋

I'm an Amazon seller who got tired of spending 30 minutes every week staring at the Business Report trying to figure out which of the 20 columns actually needed my attention.

The problem: the data is all there, but it doesn't tell you what to *do*. You can see your CTR, your CVR, your ACOS — but you have to figure out yourself which one is the real problem, what's causing it, and what the fix is.

So I built ASINInsight.

You upload the Detail Page Sales and Traffic CSV (the one you already pull from Seller Central). It checks CTR, CVR, ACOS, Buy Box percentage, sessions, and review count — ranks them by revenue impact — and gives you the single highest-priority fix with numbered action steps.

**The thing I'm most proud of:** it tells you the dollar amount you're losing each month from the problem. Not just "your CTR is low" but "your CTR of 0.12% is costing you an estimated $340/month compared to category average."

**Free plan** — 5 diagnoses/month, no credit card required.

Happy to answer any questions about the project or Amazon selling in general. If you try it and the diagnosis seems off, let me know — still tuning the edge cases.

## Response Templates (for comments on launch day)

**If someone asks about accuracy:**
> The diagnosis is rule-based, not AI-generated — it uses the same thresholds experienced Amazon consultants use (0.20% CTR floor, 8% CVR floor, 35% ACOS ceiling, etc.). It won't be right for every edge case, but it catches the most common problems reliably.

**If someone asks about data privacy:**
> Your CSV is analyzed on the server and deleted immediately after the diagnosis. Nothing is stored. The Privacy Policy at asininsight.com/privacy covers this in detail.

**If someone asks about the roadmap:**
> Next: multi-ASIN batch view (table of all ASINs with individual scores), Amazon SP-API direct integration (skip the CSV upload), and team accounts. Happy to prioritize based on what people actually need.
