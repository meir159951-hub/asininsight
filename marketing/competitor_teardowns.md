## Five Hero-Copy Patterns That Work in This Category

1. **Verb + Power + Outcome** — "Grow eCommerce Profitability with AI-Driven Automation" (Trellis) / "Sell Smarter. Scale Faster." (SellerApp). *Imperative verb, single payoff.*
2. **Reclaim-control framing** — "Your Amazon ads should answer to you." (Helium 10). *Cast Amazon as the adversary; your tool is the user's leverage.* The strongest hero in the set.
3. **Algorithm-name flex** — "the first agentic AI platform built for COSMO & Rufus discovery" (ZonGuru). *Naming the algorithm signals insider expertise.*
4. **Comparative quantification** — "20% more accurate," "18x more GMV coverage," "11 years of data" (Jungle Scout). *Three numbers stacked.*
5. **Process compression** — "From sourced to sold. Faster." (Seller 365). *Five words. Whole workflow, one promise.*

## The White-Space Map — What NOBODY Says in Their Hero

After analyzing all 10 competitor heroes, these promises are **completely unowned:**

| Unowned promise | Why it matters | ASINInsight stance |
|---|---|---|
| **"Deleted in seconds. Never stored, never shared."** | Every competitor stores your data in their cloud indefinitely; the cancellation-trap reviews show this is felt as a betrayal. | Zero-retention promise — backed by code that holds CSV in memory only. |
| **"No SP-API. No login. No card."** | Every competitor requires at least one of those three. Triple-no is unowned. | Anti-funnel positioning. |
| **"Plain English, ranked by what to fix first."** | Every competitor outputs a *score* or dashboard. Nobody promises a numbered prescription. | Differentiator on output format. |
| **"From your Sales & Traffic report."** | Nobody mentions this CSV by name in their hero. | Own the term — sellers know what file you mean. |
| **"60 seconds."** | No competitor anchors a time-to-value number on the hero. | Concrete and unowned. |
| **"Free, actually free."** | AuditMyListing throttles to 3/mo. ZonGuru's "free" is 7 days. **Nobody combines free + no-card + no-login.** | ASINInsight uniquely can. |

**Recommended hero stack combining unowned promises:**

> *"Drop your Sales & Traffic CSV. Get a numbered fix list in 60 seconds. No Amazon login. No SP-API. No card. CSV deleted instantly — never stored, never shared."*

Alternative tested-against headlines:
- *"The Amazon listing audit that doesn't need your password."*
- *"Stop guessing. Your Business Report already has the answer."*
- *"The 60-second audit your agency charges $1,500 for."*

## Funnel Mechanics — Three Patterns + the Anti-Funnel

### Pattern 1: Free trial → credit card → auto-bill (the trap)
Helium 10, Jungle Scout, ZonGuru, SellerApp's Smart Plan. *Generates the cancellation-refund complaint pile on Trustpilot/BBB. High LTV when it works, but public-review damage is enormous and growing.*

### Pattern 2: Demo gate
Trellis, Jungle Scout (homepage now). *Sales-led. Slow. Only economical at $500+/mo ACV. Locks you out of solo sellers entirely.*

### Pattern 3: No-credit-card free trial / freemium
Sellerboard (1 month, no CC), Sellerise (7 days, no CC), SellerApp freemium, AuditMyListing free tier. *Lower conversion per signup but cleaner brand and lower CAC at scale.*

### Pattern 4 (UNOWNED): Audit before signup → email → card
**This is ASINInsight's competitive opening.** Drop CSV → audit runs in browser → see the full report. *If* you want PDF / history / re-audit, **email-only signup** (no password). *If* you want >3/mo, **then** card.

> The credit-card decision moves from "before I see value" to "after I've seen value three times."

This is the **anti-funnel** — exactly the position that converts the wave of sellers fleeing the cancellation-trap reviews of Helium 10, JS, and ZonGuru.

These quotes are public on Trustpilot/BBB. They're the wedge.

> *"refusing to cancel my account after me requesting cancellation 8+ times within the last 2 years."* — Helium 10 (Trustpilot)

ASINInsight's "no card, no login, no commitment" stance isn't just a feature — it's a **defense against the exact pain those reviews describe.**

Reflect this directly in the trust strip:

🔒 No Amazon login. No SP-API. Your data never touches our servers.
🎯 Prescription, not grading. Critical issues, ranked. Plain English.
💸 One-click cancel. No retention calls. No forced renewal.

## One Watch-Out

**ZonGuru's Helix is the only competitor naming COSMO/Rufus by name.** If Amazon's algo really has shifted toward those (and the public data says it has — 274M Rufus queries/day, $10B incremental sales), ZonGuru just took the SEO insider position.

**ASINInsight's defense:** the action plan must explicitly map each fix to A10 / COSMO / Rufus signals. Otherwise ZonGuru's "agentic AI for COSMO" outpositions us on technical credibility.
