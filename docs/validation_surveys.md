## 🎯 What to Look For in Responses

### 🟢 Strong signals (proceed with confidence)

- *"Yes, every month it's like starting over"*
- *"I wish my tool remembered that I tried that already"*
- *"My agency forgets which SKUs I told them not to scale"*
- Specific dollar examples of waste
- Volunteers to be a beta customer
- Asks "when can I sign up?"

### 🟡 Weak signals (interesting but not enough)

- *"That sounds useful"*
- *"Yeah maybe"*
- *"I'd consider it"*
- Generic agreement without specifics

### 🔴 Negative signals (reframe needed)

- *"My current tool is fine"*
- *"Why would I switch?"*
- *"I don't see the value"*
- Crickets (no engagement)
- Mods removing posts
