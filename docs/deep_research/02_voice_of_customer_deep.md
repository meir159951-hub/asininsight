## 🌐 Pattern 8: Demographic / Sub-Segment Patterns

### Private-label sellers (Pro $89 ICP — Q24, Q29, Q32)

- **Behavior:** Long forum posts. Revenue figures. ROAS/ACOS numbers. Explicit P&L language.
- **Vocabulary:** Names specific tools ("Jungle Scout," "Helium 10," "Amazon Approved Partner")
- **Posture:** Analytic, measurement-fluent
- **Willing to pay for diagnosis if it's specific**

### Resellers / book sellers (NOT our ICP — Q5, Q6, Q9)

- **Behavior:** Shorter, more emotional posts
- **Cite tenure:** "15 years," "since 2005"
- **Blame:** Amazon, the economy, FBM-vs-FBA shifts
- **Less likely to pay** for ASIN-level tools (their problem is portfolio-wide)

### Veterans (15+ years) vs. newbies

| | Vet | Newbie |
|---|---|---|
| Blames | Amazon, macro | Themselves |
| Volume of post | Long | Short |
| Specificity | High | Low |
| Tool savviness | High | Low |
| Buying motion | Considers carefully | Easily swayed |

**Marketing implication:**
- To newbies: *"your listing has 7 fixable issues"*
- To veterans: *"here's the algorithmic shift hitting your category"*
