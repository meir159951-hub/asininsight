## 🥇 Channel #1: Founder-Led Direct Outreach

### Why it works for SellerCopilot
- **Solo founders MUST do this** — there's no other path to first 10 customers
- Conversion is highest when seller talks to founder directly
- Generates the case study material for future channels
- Free (just Meir's time)

### Specific tactics

**1. Warm network (Week 1)**
- Meir's existing LinkedIn connections — any Amazon sellers?
- Friends/family who know FBA sellers
- People who comment on Meir's existing content (if any)

**2. Forum-driven warm leads (Week 2-4)**
- Comment thoughtfully on 5 PPC pain threads per day in Seller Central forum
- After they reply, DM them: *"Hey, mind if I ask a follow-up question?"*
- Convert DM conversations to paid customers

**3. Founder-led demo calls (Week 4-12)**
- Once positioned, offer free 30-min calls
- Script: *"Show me your last 30 days of PPC. I'll show you what SellerCopilot would suggest. No pitch."*
- Conversion: 30-50% of demo to paid (founder-led trust)

### Benchmarks (B2B SaaS 2026)
- Greg Mercer landed Jungle Scout's first sale in 3-4 weeks via Facebook groups
- Most bootstrap founders: 6-8 weeks to first customer
- Founder handles first 30-50 sales personally before delegating

- [Bessemer Venture Partners - Direct Sales 101](https://www.forumvc.com/thought-pieces/direct-sales-101-for-early-stage-b2b-saas-founders)
- [SaaS Club - First Customers](https://saasclub.io/podcast/first-customers/)

## 🥉 Channel #3: Amazon Seller Central Forums

### Why it's underrated
- **Real sellers** (Amazon login required to post)
- **Lower moderation aggression** than Reddit
- **Permissive of helpful comments** that cite your tool at the end
- **High-density seller pain** in real-time

### Tactical playbook

**Daily routine (30 min/day):**
1. Open https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums
2. Sort by "newest" in Advertising category
3. Find 3-5 PPC pain threads
4. Write a substantive answer (not promotional)
5. Sign-off with low-key brand mention: *"Working on a tool for exactly this. DM if curious."*

**Original posts (1/week):**
- Genuine questions to the community
- Survey-style posts (the validation surveys in `docs/validation_surveys.md`)

### Expected results

| Time | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Month 1 | 10-30 thoughtful conversations |
| Month 3 | 5-15 leads in DMs |
| Month 6 | 30-50 leads accumulated |
| Year 1 | 100+ leads, 30-50 customers from this channel |

### Risk
- If post becomes too promotional → mod removes
- If DM is too aggressive → reported
- Solution: stay genuinely helpful, soft brand mentions only

## 8️⃣ Channel #8: Cold Email (Targeted)

### Only after warm channels exhausted

Cold email works only with:
1. Highly personalized messages
2. Specific trigger events (e.g., "I noticed you posted about ACOS issues last week")
3. Clear value offer

### Template (see `docs/validation_surveys.md` for variations)

```
Subject: Quick PPC question (not pitching)

Hey [Name],

I came across your post in [FBA group / Seller Central thread] about
[specific PPC issue]. Genuine question, not pitching:

When you switch PPC tools, do you find performance drops for the first
few weeks while the new tool "learns" your account?

I'm building a tool that addresses this by having persistent memory
of your account across sessions. Trying to figure out if this pain
is universal or just one I'm overthinking.

Would value 5 minutes of your honest take.

— Meir
```

### Volume
- Send 10-20/day max
- Personalize each message (no scripts)
- Expect 5-15% reply rate

### Expected results
- Year 1: 20-50 customers from cold email

### Sources
- [Woodpecker - SaaS Cold Email Guide](https://woodpecker.co/blog/saas-cold-email/)

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Channel #11: AppSumo (Agency tier LTD)

### Why consider
- One-time burst of customers + cash
- Validates Agency $199 tier
- Cap at 500 codes to avoid support overload

### Mechanism
- AppSumo offers lifetime deals at deep discount (typically 80% off)
- $199 Agency annual ($2,388/yr) → AppSumo LTD at $99-$299 one-time
- Net: $50-$150 per code after AppSumo split

### Risk
- LTD customers are not your highest-quality
- Support burden if 500 codes sold
- Brand perception of "discount product"

### Recommendation
- **Only after $5K MRR proven** (Q2-Q3 Year 2)
- Cap at 500 codes
- Agency tier only (don't LTD the Pilot/Operator tiers)

## ❌ Channels to AVOID at launch

### Cold outreach (untargeted)
- LinkedIn InMail blast: spam, low conversion
- Bought email lists: TOS violations, no quality
- Twitter mass-DM: gets account suspended

### Display ads
- Google/Facebook display: terrible B2B SaaS ROI
- Banner ads: dead channel for B2B

### Affiliate marketing
- Need product first
- Will revisit Year 2

### Influencer marketing
- $5K-$50K per influencer
- Hard to measure ROI
- Skip until $25K MRR
