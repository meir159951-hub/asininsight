# 🎯 Founder-Led Sales Playbook — First 10 Customers

> **Purpose:** A specific, tactical guide for Meir's first 10 paying customers.
> **Date:** 2026-05-11
> **Principle:** "Personal handling of every customer is essential data collection, not inefficiency."

---

## 🧭 The North Star

> **Meir personally talks to every one of the first 50 customers.**

This is not optional. It's how you:
1. Learn what's actually broken in the product
2. Hear the exact language sellers use
3. Build relationships that become referrals
4. Generate case studies for marketing

Once you have 50 customers and ~$5K MRR, you can systematize. **Not before.**

## 📅 Week-by-Week Plan

### Week 1: Validate the wedge

**Goal:** Confirm sellers actually want what you're building.

**Daily activities (2-3 hours/day):**

**Monday:**
- Post Survey #1 to Amazon Seller Central forum (text in `docs/validation_surveys.md`)
- Apply to FBA High Rollers Facebook group
- Set up LinkedIn profile properly

**Tuesday:**
- Comment on 5 PPC pain threads in Seller Central forum
- Reply to comments on your survey post (within 1 hour of each)

**Wednesday:**
- Send 5 personalized Twitter DMs to FBA voices (templates in `docs/validation_surveys.md`)
- Post 1 thoughtful insight on Twitter

**Thursday:**
- LinkedIn connection requests: 15-20 personalized to FBA sellers
- Reply to forum comments

**Friday:**
- Review week's responses
- Tag responses: 🟢 strong / 🟡 medium / 🔴 weak
- Note specific quotes for later

**Saturday/Sunday:**
- Light engagement — comments only
- Read voice of customer compendium (`docs/deep_research/02_voice_of_customer_deep.md`)
- Sharpen your understanding

**End of week 1 measure:**
- 10+ thoughtful conversations started
- 3+ "I'd pay for this" indications
- 0 customers (this is normal)

### Week 2: Get warm leads

**Goal:** Convert forum/DM conversations into first sales calls.

**Activities:**

**Monday:**
- For everyone who responded positively in Week 1, DM:
  ```
  Hey [Name], thanks for replying on the PPC tools thread.
  You mentioned [specific thing they said].

  I'm building a tool that addresses exactly this. Would you be open
  to a 20-min Zoom this week? Free, no pitch — I'll show you what I'm
  building and you tell me if it solves your problem.

  If yes — would [day/time] work?
  ```

**Tuesday-Thursday:**
- Take 2-4 calls per day with positive respondents
- Each call follows the same structure (see below)

**Friday:**
- Post FBA High Rollers survey if approved
- Continue Seller Central engagement

**End of week 2 measure:**
- 5-10 sales/discovery calls completed
- Identify 2-3 hottest prospects
- 0-1 paying customers (still early)

---

### Week 3-4: Close first 3-5 customers

**Goal:** Get your first 3-5 paying customers as design partners.

**Tactical moves:**

1. **Offer design partner pricing:** $49/mo for first 5 customers (locked for 12 months)
   - This is 45% off the future $89 price
   - In exchange: weekly 30-min check-in calls, full data sharing, public testimonial
   - **Critical: This isn't begging. This is "I want to build with you, not for you."**

2. **Close in the call:**
   - Don't ask "what do you think?"
   - Ask: "Does this solve [specific pain you both discussed]?"
   - If yes: *"Want to be one of the 5 design partners at $49/mo? I'd love to build with you for the next 12 months."*

3. **Make it easy:**
   - Payment link ready (Paddle, already in repo)
   - Onboarding email pre-written
   - First call scheduled within 24 hours of payment

**End of weeks 3-4 measure:**
- 3-5 paying design partners at $49/mo
- $147-$245 MRR
- Weekly call schedule established

---

### Week 5-12: Build with them, sell to others

**Goal:** Get to 10-15 paying customers total, generate case studies.

**Twin tracks:**

**Track A — Build with the 5 design partners:**
- Weekly 30-min call with each
- Implement their top requests within 48 hours when possible
- Document every win: "Saved $X in PPC spend in Week N"
- By Week 12: 2-3 strong case studies with permission to publish

**Track B — Sell to new prospects at full price:**
- Continue Week 1-2 motion (forums, DMs, LinkedIn)
- Now you have case study material to share
- Price: $89/mo (the real price)
- Conversion: 30-50% of demo to paid (founder-led trust)

**End of Week 12 measure:**
- 10-15 paying customers total
- 5 design partners at $49 + 5-10 new at $89
- MRR: $700-$1,150
- 1-2 published case studies

## 📞 The Discovery Call Script

### Before the call
- Read their forum posts / LinkedIn profile / DMs
- Pull up Amazon Seller Central (have an example demo account ready)
- 20 min max

### Opening (2 min)

> *"Thanks for taking the call. I'll be quick — 20 minutes max. I'd like to spend 5 minutes asking you about your PPC setup, 10 minutes showing you what I'm building, and 5 minutes seeing if it would be useful for you. Sound good?"*

### Discovery (8 min)

**Question 1:** *"Walk me through your last week of PPC management. Specifically: what did you actually do, hour by hour?"*
- Listen for: time spent, frustration points, tool gaps

**Question 2:** *"What's the one piece of context about your business you wish your current tool understood that it doesn't?"*
- THE question. Their answer is your value prop.

**Question 3:** *"What would have to be true for you to switch from [their current tool]?"*
- Listen for: deal-breakers, must-haves, pricing sensitivity

### Demo (8 min)

- **Don't show features.** Show outcomes.
- Open the demo account
- Show 1 scenario: *"Imagine SellerCopilot sees your account. Here's what it would notice in 30 seconds..."*
- Walk through 1 suggestion with reasoning
- Show the memory inspector

### Close (2 min)

> *"Two questions:*
> *1. Is this solving the pain we just talked about?*
> *2. If yes, what would prevent you from starting today?"*

If they say yes + no objections:
> *"I'm offering my first 5 customers $49/mo locked for 12 months in exchange for weekly check-ins and feedback. Want in?"*

If they hesitate:
> *"What would help you decide? Reference customer? Trial period? More demo?"*

### After the call
- Email within 1 hour: thank you + summary of what discussed + clear next step
- Add to CRM (even if it's a simple Google Sheet)

## 💬 Objection Handling

### "I already pay for Helium 10 / Adtomic. Why switch?"

> *"I'd never tell you to replace Helium 10 entirely — it does keyword research and bulk operations well. But for the strategic PPC piece, ask yourself: does Adtomic actually remember the decisions you made last month, or does it restart cold? My customers found running both for 30 days was the easiest test."*

### "How do I know your AI won't make terrible decisions?"

> *"Every change requires your one-click approval. The AI proposes — you decide. And we have a 30-day rollback on everything. Worst case, you reverse anything that doesn't work. Show me one tool that gives you that."*

### "What if you disappear / get acquired / pivot?"

> *"Fair concern. Three things: (1) Your memory data is exportable any time as plain text. (2) The architecture is built on Anthropic's stack — even if SellerCopilot pivots, that work is preserved. (3) I'm not VC-funded, so I'm not under pressure to acquire-or-die in 18 months."*

### "Price is high vs. Adtomic at $129"

> *"You're right that $89 is a real number. Let me re-frame: at $89/mo, if SellerCopilot saves you $200 in wasted PPC per month — which is conservative — your payback is 2 weeks. What's Adtomic's payback?"*

### "I want to think about it"

> *"That's fair. Can I ask — what's the specific thing that's making this not a 'yes' today? If it's price, I have a design partner offer. If it's product readiness, I can give you a 14-day trial. If it's trust, I can connect you with my early customers."*

**Never push past a real 'no.'** Move on. Most won't buy. The ones who do, will be your evangelists.

## 🎁 Design Partner Offer (Detailed)

### What they get
- $49/mo locked for 12 months (vs. $89 list)
- Direct access to founder (Meir)
- Weekly 30-min Zoom check-ins
- Feature requests implemented within 48 hours when possible
- First access to new features
- Public credit as "Design Partner" (optional)

### What you get
- $49 × 5 = $245 MRR (early revenue)
- 5 hours/week of direct customer feedback
- Permission to use them as case studies
- Most likely future testimonial source
- Their network (referrals)

### Selection criteria for design partners
- Must spend $5K+/mo on PPC (relevant ICP)
- Must be willing to commit to weekly calls
- Must be active engager (no dead leads)
- Must accept the constraint: "we're building together, things will break"

### Communicating the limit
> *"I'm taking 5 design partners total. After that, the price is $89."*

This creates urgency without manufacturing fake scarcity.

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Selling features
You'll be tempted to demo "look at this cool feature." Don't. Sellers don't buy features. They buy outcomes. Always frame as: *"this saves you 3 hours/week and $200/mo in wasted spend."*

### Mistake 2: Discounting in panic
If someone hesitates, your first instinct will be to drop price. **Don't.** Instead, ask what would help them decide. If price is the blocker, offer design partner pricing — never a random discount.

### Mistake 3: Building what they ask for vs. what they need
A customer says "I want X feature." But what they really need is solved by Y. Don't be a yes-machine. Push back: *"What's the underlying problem X solves for you?"*

### Mistake 4: Letting deals stall
If a prospect hasn't replied in 5 days, send 1 follow-up. If no reply in 12 days, move on. Don't let stalled deals waste energy.

### Mistake 5: Hiding from negative feedback
When a customer churns or rejects, schedule a 15-min exit call. Ask: *"What didn't work?"* Listen. Don't defend.

### Mistake 6: Ignoring referrals
Every happy customer should be asked: *"Who else is dealing with this pain?"* 30% of B2B SaaS growth is referral-driven in the early stages.

## 💪 Mindset

This is the hardest 90 days of your founder journey. Rules to live by:

1. **Talk to customers every day.** Not optional.
2. **Treat every "no" as data, not failure.** Note why.
3. **Don't hide behind code.** Sales > more features at this stage.
4. **Celebrate small wins.** First demo. First payment. First testimonial.
5. **Document everything.** Notes from every call → product backlog.

You're not just selling a product. You're learning what the product should be.
