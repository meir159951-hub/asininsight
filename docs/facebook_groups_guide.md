## 📋 How to Answer Membership Questions (FBA High Rollers)

When you click "Join Group", they will ask 3-5 questions. Typical ones:

### Q: "Are you an active Amazon seller?"

❌ **Don't write:** "No, I'm researching for a startup"
✅ **Write:** "Building an Amazon-focused tool, doing research to make sure I solve the right problem."

(Truth, but framed as research, not as a non-seller "outsider")

---

### Q: "What's your monthly revenue / how long selling?"

❌ **Don't write:** "I don't sell, I'm a developer"
✅ **Write:** Just be honest — *"Not currently selling. Background in building tools. Spent the last 60 days deep in seller research."*

---

### Q: "Why do you want to join?"

✅ **Write:** *"To learn from experienced sellers about the real PPC pain points and what current tools miss. I'm researching whether there's a better way to build this and I want to hear from people actually running ad spend."*

This is honest. It signals research intent (which most mods respect more than "I want to learn FBA" pitches).

---

### Q: "Helium 10 subscription?"

This one is the wildcard. Some groups require it.

**Options:**
- **If yes:** Mention your plan (Starter/Platinum/Diamond)
- **If no:** *"Not currently. Open to subscribing if the group is the right fit for my research."* (Honest, doesn't lie about being a customer)

## ⚠️ What NOT to Do

1. **Don't promote your tool in your first post.** Mods watch this carefully and remove + ban.
2. **Don't DM members en masse.** This is reported as spam, gets you banned.
3. **Don't argue with replies.** If someone says "your question is dumb" — thank them and move on.
4. **Don't repost the same question.** Once is enough. If you don't get responses, try a different group.
5. **Don't follow up by saying "I'm building X!"** unless someone EXPLICITLY asks twice.

## 🎯 Posting Strategy (After Approval)

### Wait 3-5 days before posting

- Engage with 3-5 other posts (genuine comments, no self-promotion)
- This builds "social presence" — mods see active engagement, not drive-by survey poster
- Like and react to relevant posts

### Then post the survey from `docs/validation_surveys.md` Survey 2

- Best time: Tuesday-Thursday, 10am-1pm US Eastern Time
- Worst time: Friday afternoon or weekend (drowned out)

### Reply to every comment within 24 hours

- *"Great point — what would you want it to specifically remember?"*
- *"Thanks for the honest take. Curious — does your current tool ever feel like it ignores your specific situation?"*
- Drill into specifics. Don't accept vague answers.

## 📨 If You Want to DM Specific Sellers

After people respond in the public thread, **wait until they engage 2-3 times** before DMing.

**DM template (after public conversation):**

```
Hey [name], appreciated your reply on the PPC tools thread.

You mentioned [specific thing they said]. I'd love to ask 2-3 follow-up
questions in DM if you're open — won't take more than 10 minutes,
genuinely just research for now.

No pitch, no signup, just want to understand the pain better.
```

If they respond → ask deep questions, listen, take notes.

## 🔥 Alternative: LinkedIn (often overlooked)

LinkedIn has growing Amazon seller communities. Lower noise than FB.

**Search for:**
- *"Amazon FBA seller"* in People search
- Filter: 2nd connections, Industry = E-commerce, Title contains "Amazon"
- ~500 results expected

**Outreach approach:**
- Connect with a personalized note: *"Hi, I see you sell on Amazon. Doing research on PPC pain points — happy to share what I'm learning in exchange for 5 min of your honest take."*
- ~10-15% accept rate is realistic
- Of accepts, ~30% will give you a meaningful conversation

This is slower but the **quality is much higher** than FB groups.
