### Option A: Pivot the entire site to SellerCopilot

- Replace ASINInsight homepage with SellerCopilot positioning
- Keep ASINInsight tool at `/tool` path (free entry funnel)
- All marketing → SellerCopilot

**Pros:** Clear focus, one brand, one story.
**Cons:** Loses any SEO equity ASINInsight built; existing users confused.

### Option B: Run both products in parallel

- Keep `asininsight.com` for the CSV product
- Launch `sellercopilot.com` as a separate landing
- Cross-link: ASINInsight → "Want this monthly with memory? Try SellerCopilot"

**Pros:** Lower risk, ASINInsight becomes a lead magnet for SellerCopilot.
**Cons:** Two brands to maintain, dilutes founder focus.

### Recommendation: Option B (for first 90 days), then re-evaluate

ASINInsight is a working free funnel. Don't kill it. Use it to feed SellerCopilot.

## FAQ (preempt common objections)

### Q: How is this different from Helium 10 Adtomic?
A: Adtomic gives you rules and bulk operations to manage at scale. SellerCopilot gives you a conversation. Adtomic doesn't remember the strategic decisions you've made about your business — SellerCopilot does. Different categories, different buyers.

### Q: Can it auto-apply changes like AutoPilot?
A: No, by design. Every change goes through your approval. This is also Amazon Agent Policy compliance — fully autonomous tools risk policy violations.

### Q: What happens if I cancel?
A: One-click cancel. No retention call. Your account immediately stops being charged. Your memory data is exportable if you want to keep it.

### Q: Is my data safe?
A: Memory is stored per-customer with workspace isolation. Built on Anthropic's Managed Agents infrastructure (used by Netflix, Notion, Asana). We never train models on your data.

### Q: How long until it "knows" my business?
A: First conversation captures the basics (goals, margins, strategy). Memory grows from there. Most customers report "feels like a partner" by week 4.
