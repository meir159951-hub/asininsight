Refund Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

1. Free Plan

The Free plan is available at no charge. No payment is required, and therefore no refunds apply.

2. Pro Plan — Monthly Subscription

If you subscribed to the Pro plan on a monthly basis, you may request a full refund within 7 days of your initial purchase or renewal date. After 7 days, the subscription fee for that billing period is non-refundable.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us at support@asininsight.com with:

  • Your account email address
  • The date of purchase
  • The reason for your refund request

We will process eligible refunds within 5–10 business days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

4. Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Cancellation stops future charges. You will retain access to Pro features until the end of your current billing period. No partial refunds are issued for unused time after the 7-day window.

5. Exceptions

We reserve the right to deny refund requests if we detect abuse of this policy (e.g., repeated refund requests across multiple accounts).

6. Contact Us

Questions? Reach us at support@asininsight.com.
