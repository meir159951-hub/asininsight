Questions

Pricing FAQ.

Can I cancel any time?
Yes, one click. No retention call, no email loop, no charges after the cancel timestamp. Cancel from your account settings, or email support@asininsight.com and we will close it for you the same business day. We use Paddle as merchant of record so cancellation runs through them directly. Full refund within 14 days of your first charge if SellerCopilot did not deliver. We have read the Helium 10 cancellation horror stories. We are deliberately not that.
What counts as one "diagnosis"?
One CSV upload = one diagnosis. If your CSV contains multiple ASINs, the engine analyzes the one with the worst combined score and gives you the highest-priority fix. Pilot and Operator plans connect via Amazon Ads API for live data, so the same five-rule analysis runs continuously instead of one upload at a time.
Does the free plan include the full action plan?
The free plan gives you the full CSV diagnosis: primary issue, severity, numbered action plan, revenue impact estimate, and email report delivery. The Pilot tier ($89/mo) adds the live PPC Agent: continuous analysis of your Amazon Ads account, the five-rule decision engine, decision audit trail, and the Why-this-matters cards.
Is there an annual billing option?
Not yet, but it's coming soon. Email us at support@asininsight.com if you'd like to be notified when annual billing (with a discount) becomes available.
