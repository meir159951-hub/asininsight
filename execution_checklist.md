# Outreach Execution Checklist

## Before sending anything

- Open [landing_page.html](C:\Users\meir1\ai_trading_system\amazon_seller_audit_mvp\landing_page.html)
- Replace `beta@example.com` with your real email
- Open [mock_audit_report.html](C:\Users\meir1\ai_trading_system\amazon_seller_audit_mvp\output\mock_audit_report.html)
- Read the sample report once so you can explain it simply
- Pick one outreach message from [validation_playbook.md](C:\Users\meir1\ai_trading_system\amazon_seller_audit_mvp\validation_playbook.md)

## First outreach batch

- Send to 10 Amazon sellers, not 100
- Use one message version only for the first batch
- Record every result in [outreach_tracker.csv](C:\Users\meir1\ai_trading_system\amazon_seller_audit_mvp\outreach_tracker.csv)
- Do not try to "sell" too hard
- Ask whether the problem feels real to them

## What to track

- Did they understand the offer immediately
- Did they ask to see the sample audit
- Did they say they already use another tool
- Did they describe a real bottleneck
- Did they ask when it will be ready
- Did they ignore the message

## Good signals

- "This is interesting"
- "I would use this"
- "How would it work?"
- "Can I test it on my store?"
- "We have this exact problem"

## Bad signals

- "I do not get it"
- "This sounds too broad"
- "Helium 10 already does this"
- "We do not have this problem"
- No replies after a reasonable follow-up

## Follow-up rule

- If no reply after 3 to 4 days, send one short follow-up
- Do not send more than one follow-up in this test round

## Minimum decision threshold

Keep going only if at least one of these happens:

- 3 people ask for details
- 2 people ask to test it
- 1 person says they would pay for it
