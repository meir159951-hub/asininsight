# Validation Playbook

## Goal

Test whether Amazon sellers understand and care about the offer:

`Find out why your Amazon sales stalled`

We are not testing the full product yet.
We are testing the message.

## Assets

- Landing page: [landing_page.html](C:\Users\meir1\ai_trading_system\amazon_seller_audit_mvp\landing_page.html)
- Sample audit: [mock_audit_report.html](C:\Users\meir1\ai_trading_system\amazon_seller_audit_mvp\output\mock_audit_report.html)

## Where to test

- LinkedIn messages to Amazon sellers
- Seller Facebook groups
- Reddit communities for Amazon sellers
- WhatsApp or Telegram groups if available
- Direct email to small brands selling on Amazon

## What success looks like

- People understand the offer without extra explanation
- People ask to try it
- People ask what data is needed
- At least 5 real sellers show interest

## What failure looks like

- People do not understand what the product does
- People think it sounds too broad
- People say they already get this from existing tools
- Nobody asks to try it

## Outreach message v1

Hi, I am testing a simple tool for Amazon sellers.

It is designed for sellers who have products, traffic, and ad spend, but still do not know why growth is stuck.

The idea is simple: upload store data and get one short report showing the likely bottlenecks across listing quality, conversion, ad waste, inventory risk, and reviews, plus what to fix first.

I put together a sample report here:

[Sample audit](C:\Users\meir1\ai_trading_system\amazon_seller_audit_mvp\output\mock_audit_report.html)

If this is relevant to your store, I can share beta access once the first version is ready.

## Outreach message v2

Quick question:

If you could upload your Amazon store data and get one report showing exactly why growth is stuck and what to fix first, would that be useful for your business?

I am validating a beta concept right now and can share a sample audit if you want to see it.

## Outreach message v3

I am testing a product for Amazon sellers that answers one question:

Why did sales stop growing?

Not another dashboard. A short diagnosis that points to the biggest bottlenecks first, like low conversion, ad waste, stock risk, weak reviews, or poor listing quality.

Would you want to see a sample report?

## Questions to ask interested sellers

- What is hardest to diagnose today when sales stop growing?
- Which is more painful: ads, inventory, conversion, or listing quality?
- Would you pay for a report like this?
- Would you want a one-time audit or a monthly monitor?
- What tools are you already using?

## Suggested threshold before building more

Do not expand the product until at least one of these happens:

- 5 sellers ask to try it
- 3 sellers say they would pay for it
- 1 seller asks when it will be ready

## Notes for the founder

- Keep the conversation on the pain, not on features
- Avoid saying "AI tool" too early
- Lead with the business outcome: diagnosis and prioritization
- If people do not react to the title alone, the positioning is still weak
