# Why Sales Stalled

## One-line pitch

An Amazon seller audit that shows exactly why sales have stalled and what to fix first.

## Who it is for

- Small and mid-sized Amazon sellers
- Brands with active listings and some traffic
- Sellers who feel growth is underperforming
- Teams that do not want another heavy analytics suite

## The problem

Most sellers know performance is weak, but they do not know where the real bottleneck is.

Possible causes get mixed together:

- low conversion
- weak click-through rate
- ad waste
- inventory risk
- weak reviews
- poor listing quality

That creates confusion and delays action.

## The offer

The seller uploads store data, and the system returns one report:

- why growth is stuck
- which issues matter most
- what should be fixed first

## Core promise

Stop guessing why sales are slow.
Get a prioritized diagnosis in plain English.

## Sample positioning lines

- Find out why your Amazon sales stalled
- See where your store is leaking revenue
- Diagnose growth bottlenecks before you spend more on ads
- Get one report that tells you what to fix first

## What makes it different

- Focused on diagnosis, not generic dashboards
- Built around prioritization, not raw metrics
- Simpler than large seller suites
- Stronger narrative than a basic listing score

## Likely objections

### "I already use Helium 10 or Jungle Scout"

Those tools are broad. This tool is narrower: it tells the seller what is most likely blocking growth right now.

### "How is this different from Amazon's own tools?"

Amazon exposes data and some optimization tools. This product combines weak signals across conversion, listing, ads, inventory, and trust into one prioritized diagnosis.

### "Why should I trust the report?"

The report must explain each finding clearly:

- what is wrong
- why it matters
- what to do next

## What we need to validate

- Do sellers understand the pitch quickly?
- Do they ask to try it?
- Do they say this is painful enough to pay for?
- Do they describe the same bottlenecks we think matter?

## Early validation goal

Get at least:

- 5 interested sellers
- 3 strong conversations
- 1 seller willing to test the first version
