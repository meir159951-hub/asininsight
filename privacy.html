Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026

ASINInsight ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website at asininsight.com ("Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Email address and payment details when you subscribe to a paid plan (processed securely by Paddle).
  • Usage data: Pages visited, IP address, and session duration collected automatically for security and rate-limiting purposes only.
  • Uploaded data: CSV files you upload are sent to our server for analysis. The CSV file contents themselves are processed in memory and discarded immediately after the analysis completes — we do not retain the raw row-level data. We do, however, save a brief summary of each analysis (overall score, headline, severity, ASIN count, and the primary issue area) so you can revisit your past audits from the dashboard. No product titles, sales numbers, ad spend, or any other metric values from your CSV are stored.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Service
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To send you receipts and important account notices
  • To improve and optimize our Service

3. How Your CSV Data Is Handled

When you upload a CSV file for analysis, it is transmitted securely (HTTPS) to our server where the diagnosis engine processes it. The raw CSV file contents (product titles, ad spend, sessions, and every other metric value) are held in memory only for the duration of the request and are not written to disk and not stored in any database. Once the response is returned to your browser, the row-level data is discarded.

What is persisted: a small audit summary — the overall score, a one-line headline, severity level, the number of ASINs analyzed, and the primary issue area (e.g. “ads”, “ctr”, “inventory”). This summary lets you revisit past diagnoses from the dashboard and is keyed to your session. It contains no metric values and no identifiers from your CSV.

4. Payment Processing

Payments are processed by Paddle.com Market Limited. We do not store your credit card information. Paddle's privacy policy is available at paddle.com/legal/privacy.

5. Sub-processors and Third-Country Transfers

To deliver the Service, we share specific data with the following sub-processors, each acting under a written data-processing agreement and only for the purpose listed:

  • Paddle.com Market Limited (United Kingdom) — payment processing and merchant of record. Receives your email and billing details when you subscribe.
  • Twilio SendGrid (United States) — transactional email delivery. Receives only the recipient email address and the email body when you request a report copy.
  • Anthropic PBC (United States) — LLM-powered insights for the Pro tier only when explicitly used. Receives the audit summary metadata for that single request; CSV row content is never sent. Free tier never invokes Anthropic.
  • Railway Corp. (United States) — application hosting and PostgreSQL database. Receives whatever the application stores (account email, audit summaries, session cookies).

For EU/EEA and UK data subjects, transfers to the United States rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where applicable, and on Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2) supplemented by sub-processor security commitments where it is not. You may request a copy of the relevant safeguards by emailing support@asininsight.com.

6. Cookies

We use one strictly necessary session cookie (sid) to keep you signed in and remember your subscription status, and one CSRF-protection cookie (_csrf) to prevent cross-site request forgery on form submissions. These two cookies are exempt from consent requirements under the EU ePrivacy Directive Article 5(3) because they are essential to deliver a service you have explicitly requested. We do not use advertising cookies. We do not use third-party tracking.

If you accept analytics on the cookie banner, we additionally load Google Analytics 4 with IP anonymization enabled. Decline at any time and analytics will not load.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as needed for the purpose collected:

  • Account email and subscription status: for the duration of your active subscription, plus 12 months after cancellation for tax/audit purposes, or until you request deletion.
  • Audit summaries (score, headline, severity, ASIN count, primary area): 12 months from creation, then automatically purged. You may delete individual audits from the dashboard at any time.
  • Lead emails (entered to receive a report copy): until you unsubscribe or 24 months, whichever is sooner.
  • Server access logs (IP address, user agent, request path): 30 days, used for rate-limiting and abuse investigation only.
  • Email-suppression records (unsubscribes): retained indefinitely as required to honor your suppression preference.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing support@asininsight.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR (EU and EEA Residents Only)

Note: The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to residents of the European Union (EU) and European Economic Area (EEA) only. If you are located in the EU or EEA, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 15): You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16): You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17): You have the right to request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to restrict processing (Art. 18): You have the right to request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20): You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format and to transfer it to another controller.
  • Right to object (Art. 21): You have the right to object to processing of your personal data for direct marketing or other purposes based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Our legal basis for processing your data is: (a) contract performance (to provide the Service you subscribed to), (b) legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, and service improvement), and (c) your consent for non-essential cookies.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@asininsight.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with any third party for monetary or other consideration. ASINInsight has no advertising partners and does not exchange user data with data brokers, analytics providers, or any other party for cross-context behavioral advertising.

The only third parties we share data with are essential service providers that operate strictly under our instructions: Paddle (payment processing), SendGrid (transactional email), Anthropic (LLM-powered insights for Pro tier — only when explicitly used), and our hosting provider. None of these constitute a “sale” or “share” under CCPA/CPRA.

California residents may submit a verifiable request to exercise rights under the CCPA/CPRA by emailing support@asininsight.com with the subject line “California Privacy Request.” We will respond within 45 days.

Our Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The Service is intended for business use by adults aged 18 or older.
