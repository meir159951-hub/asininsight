Questions

Everything you need to know.

What exactly is a Business Report and where do I get it?
It's a CSV file from Amazon Seller Central. Go to Reports → Business Reports → Detail Page Sales and Traffic by Child Item. Set the date range to last 30 days and click Download. The file contains your CTR, CVR, sessions, units ordered, ACOS, Buy Box %, and more — everything ASINInsight needs.
Is my data private? Do you store my CSV?
Your file is analyzed on our server and permanently deleted immediately after the diagnosis is generated. We never store your CSV, never share it, and have no access to your Amazon account. No credentials required — just the exported file.
What's the difference between the free plan and Pro?
The free plan gives you the primary issue diagnosis with severity and action steps — everything you need to identify and fix your #1 problem. Pro adds multi-ASIN batch analysis, email delivery of reports, and historical trend tracking across uploads. See full comparison →
My listing has multiple problems. Does this only show one?
By design, yes — you see the single highest-priority issue. Amazon listings often have 5–10 metrics off threshold simultaneously, but fixing them all at once makes it impossible to know what worked. ASINInsight prioritizes the one that's costing you the most revenue right now. Fix that first, re-upload next week, and it'll surface the next one.
Does this work if I don't run ads?
Yes. If you don't run ads, ACOS won't be present in your report and ASINInsight skips that check automatically. It still analyzes CTR, CVR, sessions, inventory, Buy Box %, and review count — all of which affect organic rank and conversion regardless of ad spend.
