Questions

Everything you need to know.

What exactly is a Business Report and where do I get it?
It's a CSV file from Amazon Seller Central. Go to Reports → Business Reports → Detail Page Sales and Traffic by Child Item. Set the date range to last 30 days and click Download. The file contains your CTR, CVR, sessions, units ordered, ACOS, Buy Box %, and everything ASINInsight needs.
Is my data private? Do you store my CSV?
Your file is analyzed on our server and permanently deleted immediately after the diagnosis is generated. We never store your CSV, never share it, and have no access to your Amazon account. No credentials required. Just the exported file.
What's the difference between the free plan and Pro?
The free plan gives you the primary issue diagnosis, severity level, full action steps, and email delivery of your report — everything you need to identify and fix your #1 problem. Pro adds unlimited diagnoses (vs. 3/month free), multi-ASIN batch analysis, and revenue impact estimates. See full comparison →
My listing has multiple problems. Will I see all of them?
Yes. You get a numbered action plan covering every issue we detect, ranked from critical to low severity, with the highest-leverage fix at #1. Amazon listings often have 5–10 metrics off threshold at once — we tell you the order to tackle them in so you know which fix is actually moving the needle. Re-upload next week to see what shifted.
Does this work if I don't run ads?
Yes. If you don't run ads, ACOS won't be present in your report and ASINInsight skips that check automatically. It still analyzes CTR, CVR, sessions, inventory, Buy Box %, and review count. All of these affect organic rank and conversion regardless of ad spend.
How is this different from generic AI tools that just say "spend more on ads"?
Every recommendation is keyed to YOUR ASIN's actual numbers — not boilerplate. If your conversion rate is fine and your ad spend is bleeding, ASINInsight will tell you to cut ads or pause the worst keywords, not spend more. The output reads like a senior FBA peer reviewing your data, not a generic SEO report.
How do I cancel? I've been burned by Helium 10's auto-renewal.
One click in your account settings. No retention call, no email loop, no charges after the cancel timestamp. We use Paddle as our merchant of record — they handle the cancellation flow directly. Full refund within 14 days if it didn't deliver. We hate cancellation traps too.
Will I get a generic "free audit" email funnel after I upload?
No. The full diagnosis is shown immediately on screen. Email is optional — only if you want a copy delivered. We don't drip-mail you 14 sales emails. If you upgrade to Pro, you get email reports as part of the product. If you don't, we leave you alone.
